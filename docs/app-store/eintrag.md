**Beschreibung** (1099 von 4000 Zeichen)

```text
Murmel is a quiet game for your iPad.

Glass marbles lie scattered across a sheet of paper. You roll them, group by group, to where they belong. Little by little a picture appears.

There is nothing to win and nothing to lose. No points, no moves counted, no timer, no stars, no leaderboard. You play at your own pace, alongside an audiobook or a cup of tea.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap a marble: it and its neighbors of the same color are selected.
• Tap a free hole of that color: the marbles roll there as a chain.
• No room yet? Put them on the wooden tray and bring them back later.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• 15 pictures, from the moon over the lake to the koi in the pond
• Your own photos become pictures to sort
• Detail from 16 × 16 up to 50 × 50 marbles, with pinch to zoom
• Soft generative sound that follows your device's silent mode
• Settings for empty spaces, marble look, rolling speed and tray size
• English, German, Italian, French and Spanish

WHAT IS NOT INSIDE
• No ads, no purchases, no account
• No tracking: your progress and your photos stay on your device
• No internet connection needed
```
